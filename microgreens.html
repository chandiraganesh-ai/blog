What Are Microgreens?

Microgreens are the young seedlings of vegetables and herbs, harvested just 7 to 21 days after germination, once the first true leaves appear. They are usually 2–8 cm tall and are cut just above the soil line.

🌱

Harvested Young

Picked at the seedling stage, when the plant is putting its energy into fresh, tender growth.

☀️

Grown in Light

Unlike sprouts, microgreens grow in soil or a growing mat with sunlight, so they develop real leaves and colour.

🥗

Intense Flavour

Each variety carries a concentrated taste of its mature plant — peppery radish, sweet pea, zesty mustard.

Sprouts vs Microgreens vs Mature Greens

Microgreens sit between sprouts and full-grown vegetables.

Harvest time2–5 days7–21 days40+ days

Benefits of Microgreens

A small handful adds nutrition, flavour and freshness to almost any meal.

1

Nutrient-Dense

Studies have found that many microgreens contain higher concentrations of vitamins such as C, E and K, and carotenoids, than the same weight of their mature leaves.

2

Rich in Antioxidants

They are a good source of polyphenols and other antioxidants that help protect the body's cells from oxidative stress.

3

Supports a Healthy Diet

Low in calories and high in fibre and micronutrients, they make it easy to eat more vegetables every day.

4

Fresh & Local

Grown in days and harvested close to when you eat them, microgreens reach your plate at peak freshness.

5

Easy to Grow at Home

All you need is a tray, some soil and a sunny windowsill — perfect for balconies and small apartments.

6

Sustainable

They need far less water, space and time than full-grown crops, making them an eco-friendly choice.

Microgreens are a healthy addition to a balanced diet, not a replacement for medical advice or treatment.
